Propagation Reaction Rate Coefficient (kp) Prediction

Predicts the free-radical propagation rate constant kp(T) for a vinyl monomer from its SMILES string. A geometry optimization and three single-point calculations (neutral, N+1, and N−1 electron states) are run in ORCA, their Conceptual DFT descriptors are extracted with Multiwfn, and the resulting Fukui functions and local softness values at the vinyl carbons are combined through a fitted Arrhenius-type model to give ln A, Ea, and kp(T).
